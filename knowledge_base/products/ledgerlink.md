# LedgerLink

## Overview
LedgerLink is Helix Freight's product for automated freight invoice audit and payment. It launched in 2021, and the product lead is Tanvi Joshi (Head of Product, LedgerLink).
Headline result: recovers 2.1% of audited freight spend on average.

## Key features
- invoice OCR and line-item extraction
- contract-rate matching
- duplicate-invoice detection
- GST and VAT reconciliation

## Pricing
- Starter: USD 600 per month (billed annually)
- Growth: USD 1,500 per month (billed annually)
- Enterprise: USD 4,800 per month (billed annually)
Enterprise contracts above USD 50,000 a year can negotiate custom terms.

## Technology
LedgerLink is built with Python services with a fine-tuned layout model for OCR and a rules engine written in Kotlin.

## Customers
LedgerLink currently has 2 customers under contract: Nordhavn Logistics, Medina Trading Co.
