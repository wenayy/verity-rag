# DockSense

## Overview
DockSense is Helix Freight's product for dock scheduling and yard management for warehouses. It launched in 2020, and the product lead is Lena Fischer (Head of Product, DockSense).
Headline result: average truck turnaround drops from 94 to 51 minutes.

## Key features
- dock appointment booking portal for carriers
- computer-vision gate check-in
- detention and demurrage tracking
- yard heat-map

## Pricing
- Starter: USD 700 per month (billed annually)
- Growth: USD 1,800 per month (billed annually)
- Enterprise: USD 5,200 per month (billed annually)
Enterprise contracts above USD 50,000 a year can negotiate custom terms.

## Technology
DockSense is built with TypeScript front end, Go services and an edge vision model running on NVIDIA Jetson devices.

## Customers
DockSense currently has 3 customers under contract: Atlantico Retail, Bharat Steel Movers, Oasis Mart.
