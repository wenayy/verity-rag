# RouteIQ

## Overview
RouteIQ is Helix Freight's product for route optimisation for mid-mile and last-mile fleets. It launched in 2018, and the product lead is Kabir Rao (Head of Product, RouteIQ).
Headline result: customers report 14% fewer kilometres driven on average.

## Key features
- vehicle routing with time windows
- dynamic re-routing when traffic or orders change
- driver app with proof of delivery
- carbon-emissions reporting per route

## Pricing
- Starter: USD 900 per month (billed annually)
- Growth: USD 2,400 per month (billed annually)
- Enterprise: USD 6,500 per month (billed annually)
Enterprise contracts above USD 50,000 a year can negotiate custom terms.

## Technology
RouteIQ is built with Python and Rust solver services on Kubernetes, with a Google OR-Tools fallback.

## Customers
RouteIQ currently has 4 customers under contract: Kaveri Foods, Gulfstar Distribution, Saffron eCom, Deccan Agro.
