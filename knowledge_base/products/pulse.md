# Pulse

## Overview
Pulse is Helix Freight's product for real-time shipment visibility and cold-chain monitoring. It launched in 2023, and the product lead is Omar Haddad (Head of Product, Pulse).
Headline result: predicts arrival within 22 minutes for 90% of shipments.

## Key features
- multi-carrier tracking API
- IoT temperature and humidity sensors
- predictive ETA
- exception alerts over WhatsApp and email

## Pricing
- Starter: USD 500 per month (billed annually)
- Growth: USD 1,300 per month (billed annually)
- Enterprise: USD 4,100 per month (billed annually)
Enterprise contracts above USD 50,000 a year can negotiate custom terms.

## Technology
Pulse is built with Elixir ingestion pipeline feeding TimescaleDB, with the ETA model served from a FastAPI service.

## Customers
Pulse currently has 3 customers under contract: Tejas Pharma, Lusitania Cold Chain, Helvetia Parcel.
